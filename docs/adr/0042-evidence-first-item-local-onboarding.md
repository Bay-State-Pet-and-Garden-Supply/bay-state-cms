# Evidence-first, item-local onboarding from sparse register data

**Status:** accepted design — owner-confirmed after the architecture interview; runtime cutover and live activation are not claimed or authorized by this record.

**GitHub record:** [Governing specification and implementation tickets #362](https://github.com/Bay-State-Pet-and-Garden-Supply/bay-state-cms/issues/362); [published ADR](https://github.com/Bay-State-Pet-and-Garden-Supply/bay-state-cms/issues/362#issuecomment-6296559539).

Onboarding must prepare rich, evidence-supported listings from only a UPC, abbreviated Register Name, and Price. We choose an evidence-first, item-local workflow: structured sources and static/profile extraction first, bounded search/browser collection for specific gaps, and per-item acceptance of attributable facts without a reusable-profile prerequisite. Each SKU owns its readiness; shared fetching, model batches, and Curation Cohorts are optimizations, not authorities that hold unrelated items.

## Decision

- Separate source authority from collection method: manufacturer, distributor, retailer corroboration, and operator-attested evidence have different admission rules; using a browser never establishes authority. Search results are untrusted candidates. The imported price remains commercial authority, not product-match evidence.
- Accept browser-collected facts only after source authority, exact sellable-item/variant binding, provenance/currentness, conflict, and applicable rights checks. Manufacturer pages need not expose a UPC if the existing conservative name/variant policy establishes the match. Ambiguity remains unresolved; neither confidence nor repeated syndicated claims resolves it.
- Keep **Browser Investigation** as domain/profile tooling with untrusted proposal evidence. Its results do not become per-item facts. Profile publishing retains its health, validation, and blind-holdout requirements; the new per-item fallback is a separate admitted route, not profile activation or relabeled manual transcription.
- Use finite, versioned **Listing Completeness Standards** per canonical Product Classification. A provisional pair may guide acquisition; final reviewed classification remains authoritative under [ADR 0041](0041-single-product-classification-contract.md). Checklist changes and corrected pairs invalidate affected requirements and dependent decisions.
- Permit explicit, item-specific **Listing Completeness Exceptions** for designated non-critical requirements, with actor, reason, and exact input/checklist revision. No automatic waiver or invented substitute. Identity/variant resolution, image rights, required safety information, and classification-required values/configuration cannot be waived.
- Keep TypeSafe as a bounded judgment tool, application code as execution/budget/deterministic-check owner, and a separate generative model as the writer of **Grounded Listing Copy** from accepted facts only. The writer cannot create facts, resolve conflicts, or fill omitted values. Every included material claim remains subject to support/contradiction checks.
- Retailer pages may corroborate attributable basic specifications after exact-item matching. They are not official pages and supply neither reusable prose nor images by default. Sensitive safety, ingredient, nutrition, dose, and medical claims require suitable authoritative evidence; retailer consensus is not that evidence.
- Replace cohort-owned completion/publication authority for independent listings with item-owned, frozen, lease-guarded authority. Do not merely remove parent gates while leaving parent-owned outputs writable. Preserve immutable history, currentness, deduplicated model work, reviewed-only classification, and genuine multi-SKU family consistency groups.
- Preserve optional Product Family review, cross-batch/catalog membership, existing Main Products, real purchasable new mains, deterministic PF7 labels, native Subproducts preservation, strict Git approval/export groups, and disabled new products. Family deferral never requires a standalone item to wait.

## Tradeoff

We reject browser-first research for every SKU, mandatory profile setup before every fallback item, and unrestricted browsing agents: they repeat site-navigation work or move the burden back to the operator. We also reject sparse listings declared complete merely because naming/classification passed, indefinite automatic research, and fabricated completeness. The chosen route adds an explicit item-evidence/exception contract and enforceable acquisition controls; whether it reduces total work remains an empirical question, not a TypeSafe performance claim.

## Precise effect on earlier decisions

- [ADR 0013](0013-cohort-centric-type-first-curation.md): supersedes cohort-owned title/page completion authority and unrelated-member readiness barriers for new item-owned execution. Frozen inputs, leases/CAS, review authority, and immutable history remain required; Product Families do not inherit Curation Cohort authority.
- [ADR 0032](0032-serp-deprecation-indexed-sitemaps.md): supersedes the platform-wide prohibition on external search only for the new approved, bounded acquisition fallback. Indexed/sitemap retrieval remains preferred. Retired SERP code/caches are not restored; local-only policy still denies cloud search.
- [ADR 0033](0033-bounded-curation-assistants-staged-authority.md): supersedes its blanket exclusion of bounded per-item browser acquisition and its cohort-owned execution constraint where they conflict with this target. It does not create a generic agent runtime or relax classification qualification, model policy, or review gates.
- [ADR 0038](0038-browser-agents-investigate-deterministic-adapters-extract.md): preserves the investigation-only contract and profile governance, but supersedes its blanket prohibition on a separately governed per-product browser-assisted route. Investigation observations still cannot be directly admitted as product evidence.
- [ADR 0040](0040-name-first-official-page-identity.md): preserves conservative name/variant matching, official-domain authority, candidate/confirmed URL distinction, and source revalidation. Healthy-profile setup is no longer a prerequisite for the new per-item fallback; broader retrieval never promotes a retailer to an official source.
- [ADR 0041](0041-single-product-classification-contract.md): remains binding. Acquisition may supply accepted own-SKU facts to frozen classifier input; the classifier itself does not fetch, enrich, inherit sibling facts, or generate listing content.

## Delivery and activation

The [governing specification](../plans/onboarding-typesafe-expansion-spec.md) replaces the former TypeSafe-expansion target at its existing path; the [implementation plan](../plans/evidence-first-onboarding-implementation-plan.md) maps the complete cutover. Keep the six pipeline stages and existing worker/repository/review/Git/codec seams; supplemental acquisition does not move items backwards or rewrite distributor/official source URLs.

No live spending, store mutation, or activation follows from design approval. Activation requires reviewed checklists and exception eligibility, enforceable per-item/batch limits, approved provider/data sharing, demonstrated browser isolation/network/navigation, and a same-population real-batch comparison of exact-item correctness, rich-listing completion, operator effort, and full acquisition cost. Unknown cost remains unknown. Existing investigation rendering is deferred by default; a separate rendered-worker implementation or passing relay/unit tests is not production proof. Rollback is disable-to-unresolved/manual or code revert, not a maintained legacy orchestration path.
