## Context & Owner Mandate

Following the qualification of TypeSafe Jev for Primary Product Types (#297), Controlled Single- & Multi-Value Attributes (#298, #300), and Category Pages across single items and cohorts (#299, #301) under #302, the owner explicitly directed consolidation on TypeSafe Jev rather than retaining previous classification-engine iterations.

TypeSafe Jev becomes the sole **model-backed Curation decision provider** for Product Types, controlled Product Attributes, and Category Pages. Deterministic domain behavior (reviewed facts, exact canonical/alias identity, configured invariants, Attribute Profiles, evidence constraints, verified Category Page identities, frozen cohort ownership, and human review in the review drawer) remains the core application spine.

Shared AI Compute connections/providers remain available for still-supported non-classification workloads (naming, descriptions, OCR, Discovery, Profile tooling, Store Manager operations).

## Post-Cutover Invariants

1. **Jev-Only Model-Backed Curation:** Only `systemone` transport is supported for Primary Product Type, Controlled Attributes, and Category Pages. Non-systemone transports produce attributable reviewable abstentions, never hidden chat fallbacks.
2. **Deterministic Precedence:** Reviewed facts, exact alias matches, brand shortcuts, and configured invariants continue to take precedence before model dispatch.
3. **No Uncalibrated Self-Confidence Clamping:** Chat confidence clamps (`Math.max(0.35, Math.min(0.85, ...))`) and margin guessing are deleted. Calibrated probabilities from TypeSafe Jev are stored truthfully alongside vendor concentration confidence.
4. **Governed Settings Migration:** Settings routes reject non-systemone transports for Curation stages with an explicit unsupported-route error. Existing local-only/Ollama policies are never silently switched to cloud Jev.
5. **Non-Classification Workload Isolation:** AI Compute connections for VLM packaging OCR, product naming consolidation, Discovery, and Store Manager tools remain operational and untouched.
