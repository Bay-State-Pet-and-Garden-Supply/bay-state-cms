# Standing boundary rules (owner-settled 2026-10-07)

Supplements the leash-clip rule (accessory-by-what-it-is,
adjudication-brief-leash-clip.md). Apply to all future batches;
amendments re-label prior gold with status notes (done: batch-1 idx29,
batch-2 idx35).

## 1. Parts excluded

Non-complete goods are NO-FIT even when they serve feeding directly:
replacement flower ports, ant moats, and equivalent components are not
instances of the feeder type. (Batch-1 R30 admitted under the old
reading; amended to NO-FIT. Batch-2 R26 sets the rule.)

## 2. Car-restraint requires travel-system membership

car-restraint covers goods whose function is travel safety as part of a
vehicle restraint system (tethers anchoring to safety systems, booster
seats built around tethers). Walking leashes — including retractable
tapes used on neighborhood walks — are collars-leashes regardless of
brand travel marketing. (Batch-2 idx35 amended accordingly; idx34
Swivel Tether stays car-restraint.)

## 3. Completeness decides food over treats

A complete-and-balanced labeled ration is food (fish-food, cat-food-wet,
dog-food-*) even in morsel/treat form factors; treats are non-complete
rewards. Package language, not shape, is the test. (Batch-2 R34
recorded cat-food-wet over the treats call.)
